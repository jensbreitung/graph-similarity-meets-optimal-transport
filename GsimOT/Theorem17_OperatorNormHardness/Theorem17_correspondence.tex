\begin{center}{\Large\bfseries Theorem 17: inapproximability for the operator $p$-norms}\\[4pt]

{\small Correspondence between the paper and the Lean~4/Mathlib formalisation}\end{center}

\noindent\textbf{Paper.} J.~Breitung, T.~Gervens, M.~Grohe. \emph{Graph Similarity Meets Optimal Transport} (file \texttt{Graph\_Similarity\_Meets\_Optimal\_Transport.pdf} in the repository). Statement, equation, page and line numbers refer to this PDF; \texttt{docs/NUMBERING.md} lists the Lean files and declarations of all numbered statements.\par\smallskip

The folder \texttt{GsimOT/Theorem17\_OperatorNormHardness} formalises Eq.~(45), Lemma~53, Corollary~54 and the reduction of Theorem~17 (p.~12, proof in Appendix~G, pp.~54--57) from Three-Partition with Claims~55 and~56 and Eq.~(46). It gives Theorem~4\,(2). Shared notions come from \texttt{GsimOT/Common}; they are listed at the end.\par\paragraph{Checking.} Run \texttt{bash build.sh} in the repository root (see \texttt{README.md}). The script builds the library and runs \texttt{scripts/CheckAxioms.lean}, which must report only the standard axioms \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice}, \texttt{Quot.sound}: no \texttt{sorry} and no additional axioms.

\paragraph{Main statement in Lean.} For an instance $(A,a_1,\dots,a_{3m})$ of Three-Partition, the graphs $G$ and $H$ arise from the
union of paths on $a_i$ vertices and the union of $m$ cycles on $A$ vertices by attaching $M$ leaves to every
vertex. Let $1\le p\le\infty$.
\lean{GsimOT.Hardness.ThreePartition.dOT_le_one_of_isYes}: for a YES-instance with all $a_i\ge2$,
$d^{\mathrm{OT}}_p(G,H)\le1$.
\lean{GsimOT.Hardness.ThreePartition.lt_dOT_of_not_isYes}: for a NO-instance with all $a_i\ge3$, $0<\varepsilon\le1$
and $M$ as in Eq.~(46), $d^{\mathrm{OT}}_p(G,H)>2^{b(p)}-\varepsilon$.

\section*{Notes on the formalisation}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item Only the correctness of the reduction is formalised: for the constructed graphs, YES-instances have a small and NO-instances a large distance. The complexity side (polynomial running time, NP-hardness of Three-Partition, H\aa{}stad's bound for MaxCut) is not formalised. The results of Section~4 and their shared gadgets are in the namespace \lean{GsimOT.Hardness}.
\item Theorem~17 is proved with the bound $2^{b(p)}-\varepsilon$ of its statement. The paper fixes $0<\varepsilon^*<\sqrt2-1$ (p.~54, l.~1728), so that $2^{b(p)}-\varepsilon^*>1$ (l.~1729--1730); the Lean statement holds for all $0<\varepsilon\le1$, which includes this range.
\item Lemma~53 is proved without the duality of Lemma~19 (Appendix~A): $A^Qx$ is the $Q$-average of $B_p\tilde x$ with $\tilde x(v,w)=Q(v,w)^{1/p}x(v)$ (the paper's $\tilde x$), and the averaging is an $\ell^p$ contraction by Jensen. Corollary~54 uses the test vectors $e_v$ and a normalised sign vector on the column support instead of $\|A\|_p=\|A^\top\|_{p^*}$.
\item YES-instances: the paper observes that $A_H-A^\pi_G$ is the adjacency matrix of $3m$ disjoint edges, so $\|A^\pi_G-A_H\|_p=1$ (p.~55, l.~1736--1737); the formalisation bounds it by $1$ by the Schur test (all absolute row and column sums are at most $1$).
\item Claim~56 takes $|N_H(w)\cap N_H(w')|\le1$ as a hypothesis; it is discharged with $A\ge7$ in the proof of the theorem.
\item Remark~62 (other operator $p\to q$-norms, p.~62) is not formalised.
\end{enumerate}
