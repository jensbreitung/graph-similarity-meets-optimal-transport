\begin{center}{\Large\bfseries Theorem 18: hardness of approximating the optimal-transport cut distance}\\[4pt]

{\small Correspondence between the paper and the Lean~4/Mathlib formalisation}\end{center}

\noindent\textbf{Paper.} J.~Breitung, T.~Gervens, M.~Grohe. \emph{Graph Similarity Meets Optimal Transport} (file \texttt{Graph\_Similarity\_Meets\_Optimal\_Transport.pdf} in the repository). Statement, equation, page and line numbers refer to this PDF; \texttt{docs/NUMBERING.md} lists the Lean files and declarations of all numbered statements.\par\smallskip

The folder \texttt{GsimOT/Theorem18\_CutHardness} formalises the reduction of Theorem~18 (p.~13, l.~443--444; proof in Appendix~G, pp.~57--62) from MaxCut with Claims~58--61 and Eq.~(47). It gives Theorem~4\,(3) up to the complexity side. Shared notions come from \texttt{GsimOT/Common}; they are listed at the end.\par\paragraph{Checking.} Run \texttt{bash build.sh} in the repository root (see \texttt{README.md}). The script builds the library and runs \texttt{scripts/CheckAxioms.lean}, which must report only the standard axioms \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice}, \texttt{Quot.sound}: no \texttt{sorry} and no additional axioms.

\paragraph{Main statement in Lean.} \lean{GsimOT.Hardness.maxcut_dOTCut_bounds_paper}: for every $0<\delta<1$ and every graph $F$ on $n$ vertices
with an edge, the graphs $G,H$ obtained from the base graphs $G',H'$ by attaching
$\ell_i=i(n^2+n)\lceil1600n^6/\delta\rceil$ leaves to the $i$-th vertex satisfy
\[ 2(1-\delta)\,\mathrm{maxcut}(F)\le d^{\mathrm{OT}}_\square(G,H)\le2\,\mathrm{maxcut}(F). \]
\lean{GsimOT.Hardness.maxcut_dOTCut_bounds_eq47}: the case $\delta=1/34$, Eq.~(47):
$\tfrac{33}{17}\,\mathrm{maxcut}(F)\le d^{\mathrm{OT}}_\square(G,H)\le2\,\mathrm{maxcut}(F)$.

\section*{Notes on the formalisation}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item Only the correctness of the reduction is formalised: for the constructed graphs, YES-instances have a small and NO-instances a large distance. The complexity side (polynomial running time, NP-hardness of Three-Partition, H\aa{}stad's bound for MaxCut) is not formalised. The results of Section~4 and their shared gadgets are in the namespace \lean{GsimOT.Hardness}.
\item The base graphs $G',H'$ are built in \texttt{BaseGraphs.lean} from the [AN06] matrix as described in the proof of Claim~59 (p.~59, l.~1885--1891): the vertices are the signed edges $e^\pm$ and the vertices of $F$; for $e=uv$, $u<v$, $G'$ has the edges $e^+u$, $e^-v$ and $H'$ the edges $e^+v$, $e^-u$. \lean{cutNorm_anG_sub_anH} proves $\|A_{G'}-A_{H'}\|_\square=2\,\mathrm{maxcut}(F)$, and the facts on $G',H',F$ that the proof uses are proved: maximum degree at most $n-1$ (p.~59, l.~1885--1892), $n'=2m+n\le n^2$ (p.~62, l.~1969) and $\mathrm{maxcut}(F)\le n^2/4$ (p.~59, l.~1880).
\item \lean{maxcut_dOTCut_bounds_paper} is the reduction with the paper's leaf counts $i(n^2+n)\lceil1600n^6/\delta\rceil$; \lean{maxcut_dOTCut_bounds_eq47} is its case $\delta=1/34$, Eq.~(47). \lean{maxcut_dOTCut_bounds} uses $n'$ in place of $n$ and needs neither the degree bound nor $n'\le n^2$.
\item Claims~58--61 are proved for general parameters ($M$, $d$, $\eta$, $\tau$, $\rho$, $\alpha$); the thresholds on $k$ are checked once, in \lean{le_dOTCut_addLeaves}, with the paper's $\alpha=\delta/(200n^4)$, $\eta=\alpha/2$, $\tau=\alpha/(4n^2)$ and $k=\lceil1600n^6/\delta\rceil$. Claim~60 is split by the order of $\ell_i$ and $\ell_j$. \lean{claim61} bounds $\|B(G,H,Q)\|_\square$ from below by $\|A_{G'}-A_{H'}\|_\square-2\alpha|V(G')|^2$; \lean{claim61_delta} derives Claim~61 from it.
\end{enumerate}
