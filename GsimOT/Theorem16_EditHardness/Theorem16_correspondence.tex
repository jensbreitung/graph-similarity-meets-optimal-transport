\begin{center}{\Large\bfseries Theorem 15: hardness of the optimal-transport edit distance}\\[4pt]

{\small Correspondence between the paper and the Lean~4/Mathlib formalisation}\end{center}

\noindent\textbf{Paper.} J.~Breitung, T.~Gervens, M.~Grohe. \emph{Graph Similarity Meets Optimal Transport} (file \texttt{Graph\_Similarity\_Meets\_Optimal\_Transport.pdf} in the repository). Statement, equation, page and line numbers refer to this PDF; \texttt{docs/NUMBERING.md} lists the Lean files and declarations of all numbered statements.\par\smallskip

The folder \texttt{GsimOT/Theorem16\_EditHardness} formalises the reduction of Theorem~15 (p.~12, proof in App.~G, pp.~50--51) from Three-Partition. It gives Theorem~4\,(1). Shared notions come from \texttt{GsimOT/Common}; they are listed at the end.\par\paragraph{Checking.} Run \texttt{bash build.sh} in the repository root (see \texttt{README.md}). The script builds the library and runs \texttt{scripts/CheckAxioms.lean}, which must report only the standard axioms \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice}, \texttt{Quot.sound}: no \texttt{sorry} and no additional axioms.

\paragraph{Main statement in Lean.} \lean{GsimOT.Hardness.ThreePartition.dOTEdit_le_iff}: for an instance $(A,a_1,\dots,a_{3m})$ of
Three-Partition with all $a_i\ge2$, the union $F_1$ of paths on $a_i$ vertices and the union $F_2$ of $m$ paths
on $A$ vertices satisfy
\[ d^{\mathrm{OT}}_{\mathrm{ed}}(F_1,F_2)\le4m\iff\text{the instance is a YES-instance}. \]

\section*{Notes on the formalisation}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item Only the correctness of the reduction is formalised: for the constructed graphs, YES-instances have a small and NO-instances a large distance. The complexity side (polynomial running time, NP-hardness of Three-Partition, H\aa{}stad's bound for MaxCut) is not formalised. The results of Section~4 and their shared gadgets are in the namespace \lean{GsimOT.Hardness}.
\item $d^{\mathrm{OT}}_{\mathrm{ed}}$ is \lean{GsimOT.dOTEdit}, the definition Theorem~6 uses. The paper argues with blowups via $d^{\mathrm{OT}}_{\mathrm{ed}}=d^{\bullet}_{\mathrm{ed}}$, cited from [Gro25, Theorem~3.10] (p.~50, l.~1585--1586). The formalisation works directly with doubly stochastic matrices: the same edge count with $b/k^2$ replaced by $\mathrm{lost}(Q)/2$, where the paper's approximation by $\gamma=1/(2mA)$ becomes exact because the minimum is attained (\lean{GsimOT.exists_dOTEdit_eq}). So nothing is cited.
\item The formalisation assumes $a_i\ge2$ (\lean{GsimOT.Hardness.ThreePartition.dOTEdit_le_iff}); the shifted version \lean{GsimOT.Hardness.ThreePartition.dOTEdit_shift_le_iff} holds for every instance.
\item Proposition~57 (p.~57; membership in NP, exact blowup sizes) is not formalised.
\end{enumerate}
